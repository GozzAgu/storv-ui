## 1. What Storvv is

Storvv is a retail management app for shops and businesses with one or more locations. It keeps track of:

- **What you have in stock**: products organised into categories, with serial numbers or quantities.
- **What you sell**: every sale is recorded as a receipt, with items, payment method and customer.
- **Who owes you money**: sales paid in part stay open until the balance is paid.
- **Who your customers are**: built automatically from your sales.
- **Your team**: staff accounts with exactly the access you choose.
- **Your branches**: each location keeps its own stock, staff and sales, and you can move stock between them.
- **How the business is doing**: reports on revenue, best sellers, busy hours and profit.

Storvv works in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and as an app on iPhone and Android. It supports light and dark mode.

## 2. Words you will see

| Word | What it means |
| ---- | ------------- |
| **Owner** | The person who created the Storvv account. Shown in the app as "Admin" or "Super admin". The owner can do everything. |
| **Staff** | A team member the owner has added. Staff only see and do what the owner allows. |
| **Branch** | One shop or location. Stock, staff and sales are kept separately for each branch. Almost every screen shows data for the branch currently selected. |
| **Category** | A group of products, such as "Phones" or "Perfumes". Each category has its own columns (for example Brand, Colour, Serial number). |
| **Subcategory** | A category inside another category, for example "Corolla" and "Camry" inside "Toyota". |
| **Product** | One row of stock inside a category. |
| **Serial-tracked** | A category where every unit has its own serial number and its own row. Used for phones, laptops, cars and similar items. |
| **Quantity-tracked** | A category where one row holds a stock count, for example "24 bottles". |
| **Sale / Receipt** | A record of something sold. Each sale gets a receipt number like REC-123456. |
| **Balance due** | A sale where the customer paid a deposit and still owes the rest. The goods are held for them until they pay in full. |
| **Swap-in** | A trade-in: the customer gives you an item as part payment for a sale. |
| **Buyback** | You buy an item from a customer and put it into your stock. |
| **Stock loan** | You lend serial-tracked items to a reseller until they sell them or bring them back. |
| **Transfer** | Moving stock from one branch to another. |
| **Low stock** | Items whose quantity is at or below your low-stock alert level (10 by default). |

## 3. Plans and what they include

Storvv has three plans. You can change plan at any time from **Settings → Account & workspace → Plan & billing**.

| | Storvv Micro | Storvv Medium | Storvv Enterprise |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Branches | 1 | 2 | Unlimited |
| Departments per branch | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Staff per branch | 2 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Inventory, sales, refunds, customers | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Quick sale and barcode scanning | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Buybacks | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Team and staff permissions | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Reports and exports | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Activity history | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Sales leads | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Customer balance (credit ledger) | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Duplicate a category or product | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Transfers between branches | – | – | ✓ |
| Copy categories from another branch | – | – | ✓ |

**If you cancel a paid plan,** you keep it until the end of the period you paid for, then the account moves to Storvv Micro. Your data is kept. If you have more branches than Micro allows, your oldest branch stays available and the others are hidden until you upgrade again.

When a screen is not included in your plan, the owner sees **"Available on Storvv Medium"** (or Enterprise) with a **View plans** button. Staff see **"Not available on this workspace"**.

## 4. Getting started

### 4.1 Create your account

1. Go to the Storvv sign-up page and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter **Your business name**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm password**.
   - Your password needs at least 12 characters, one number and one uppercase letter. A strength meter shows Weak, Fair, Good or Strong as you type.
3. Tick **I accept the terms and privacy policy**.
4. Select **Create account**.
5. You will see **"Check your email"**. Storvv has sent you a verification link.

You start on the free Storvv Micro plan.

### 4.2 Verify your email

1. Open the email from Storvv (check your spam folder if you can't find it).
2. Select the verify link. You will see **"Your email is verified"**.
3. Select **Continue to sign in**.

If you sign in before verifying, Storvv shows **Verify your email**. Use **Resend the link** to get a new email, then **I've verified my email** once you have clicked it.

### 4.3 Sign in

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**. Tick **Remember me** if this is your own device.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. If you have turned on two-factor authentication, enter the 6-digit **Authentication code** from your authenticator app and select **Verify and continue**.

On the iPhone and Android apps you can also turn on **Use Face ID/Touch ID next time on this device**.

### 4.4 Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email** and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and select the link.
4. Enter a **New password**, **Confirm password**, then select **Update password**.
5. Select **Continue to sign in** and sign in with your new password.

### 4.5 Set up your store (first sign-in, owner only)

The first time you sign in, Storvv asks three quick questions. You can change all of them later in Settings.

**Step 1 of 3: Currency and country**

1. Choose your **Currency**. Every price, sale and report uses it.
2. Choose your **Country**. This sets how dates, times and numbers are shown.
3. Select **Continue**.

**Step 2 of 3: How do you run your business?**

- **Just me**: a simpler app for running the business yourself. Team and multi-branch tools are hidden until you turn them on.
- **Growing business**: the full app with team, branches and advanced tools (as your plan allows).

Pick one and select **Continue**. (**Skip for now** chooses Growing business.)

**Step 3 of 3: Add your head branch**

1. Enter your **Head branch**, for example the city or area of your main shop.
2. Optionally add an **Address**, **Phone**, **Email** and **What you sell**. These appear on receipts.
3. Select **Finish setup**.

Storvv creates your first branch and opens your Overview.

### 4.6 Your first steps

On the Overview, a **Get set up** checklist shows what to do next:

1. **Create an inventory category**: group products so your team can find stock quickly.
2. **Add your first product**: open a category and add at least one item.
3. **Record a sale**: create your first receipt.
4. **Invite a team member**: add staff so they can sign in (hidden in "Just me" mode).

Each step has a button that takes you to the right place. The checklist disappears when you finish, or select **Dismiss** to hide it.

A short guided tour also appears the first time. Use **Next**, **Previous** or **Skip**. You can replay it any time from **Help → Replay tour** or **Profile → Dashboard tour**.

### 4.7 Staff: your first sign-in

If your manager has added you as staff:

1. You will receive an email with your sign-in details, or your manager will give you a temporary password.
2. Sign in with your email and the temporary password.
3. Storvv asks you to **Set your password**. Enter the **Temporary password**, a **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
4. Select **Update password**. You go straight to the Overview.

## 5. Finding your way around

### 5.1 The sidebar

On a computer, the menu is on the left. On a tablet or phone, tap the menu button (or **More** in the bottom bar) to open it.

**Main**
- **Overview**: today's numbers and what needs attention.
- **Inventory**: your categories and products.
- **Sales**: every sale, plus balances still owed.
- **Customers**: everyone you've sold to.
- **Reports**: charts and exports (Medium and Enterprise).

**Operations**
- **Transfers**: move stock between branches (Enterprise).
- **Stock loans**: items lent to resellers (Enterprise).
- **Buybacks**: items bought from customers.
- **Sales leads**: people who might buy (Medium and Enterprise).

**Organization**
- **Team**: departments and staff (owner only).
- **Branches**: your locations (owner only).
- **Activity**: who changed what (Medium and Enterprise).

**Bottom**
- **Help** and **Settings**.

To make the sidebar narrower on a computer, select **Collapse sidebar** at the bottom. It shrinks to icons; hover an icon to see its name. Select **Expand sidebar** to bring it back.

### 5.2 The bottom bar (phones)

On phones, the bottom bar has **Overview**, **Inventory**, **Sales**, **Customers** and **More**. **More** opens the full menu.

### 5.3 Switching branch

The branch you are working in is shown at the top of the sidebar.

1. Select the branch box.
2. Choose another branch from the list. The current one has a tick.
3. Every screen now shows that branch's data.

**Manage branches** at the bottom of the list opens the Branches page. Staff see their assigned branch but cannot switch.

### 5.4 Search

1. Select the search bar at the top (**Search products, sales, customers**), or press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows.
2. Start typing. Storvv searches sales (receipt number, customer, items), inventory categories, customers, leads, departments and staff.
3. Use the chips (**All**, **Sales**, **Inventory**, **Customers**, **Leads**, **Departments**, **Staff**) to narrow results. **Filters** adds a date range and a sale status.
4. Use the arrow keys and press **Enter** to open a result, or click it.

You can save a search you use often: open **Manage** under Recent, then **Save current search**.

### 5.5 Storvv Assistant

Select **Ask AI** in the top bar to open the Storvv Assistant. Ask it how to do something in Storvv, for example "How do I copy categories from another branch?". It explains screens, permissions and workflows. It cannot see your actual stock, sales or customers, and its answers are guidance only.

### 5.6 Refresh

Select the refresh button in the top bar to reload the current screen's data. On the phone app you can also pull down to refresh.

### 5.7 Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows a count of unread notifications about sales, inventory and team activity.

1. Select the bell to see them. Use **Inbox** and **Read** to switch lists.
2. Select a notification to open the related sale, lead, category or department.
3. Select **Mark all read** to clear the count, or **View all** for the full page.

### 5.8 Your account menu

Select your initials or photo in the top right to:

- See your role, plan and branch.
- Open **Profile**, **Settings**, **Notifications** or **Help center**.
- Change **Appearance** to **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **Sign out**.

## 6. Overview (your home screen)

The Overview shows how the selected branch is doing today.

**At the top**
- A greeting with your name, the branch and your role.
- A one-line summary, for example "3 sales · ₦45,000 today · 2 balances open".
- **Add product** and **New sale** buttons.

**Four number cards** (select any card to go to the related page)
- **Revenue**: all completed sales, with the change over the last 15 days.
- **Sales today**: number of sales today and their value.
- **Outstanding**: total money still owed on balance-due sales.
- **Low stock**: how many items are running low.

**Revenue chart**: switch between **Daily** (last 30 days), **Weekly** (last 12 weeks) and **Monthly** (last 12 months).

**Needs attention**: up to five things to act on, such as outstanding balances, low stock, or a quiet week with no sales. Each has a link to fix it. When there's nothing to do, it says **All clear**.

**Recent sales**: your last five sales. Select one to see its details, record a payment or print it. **View all** opens the Sales page.

**Low stock**: the items running lowest. Select **Export** to download a reorder list in Excel.

## 7. Inventory

Inventory is organised in two levels: **categories** (and optional subcategories) contain **products**.

### 7.1 The Categories page

Open **Inventory** from the menu. You will see:

- **Numbers** at the top: Categories, Products, Total value, Total profit (owner only) and Low stock.
- **All categories** and **Low stock** tabs.
- A **Search categories** box.
- **Sort** by Name, Products or Date.
- A department filter (owner only).
- **Grid view** or **Table view**.

Each category card shows its name, how many products or subcategories it has, its **Stock value**, and badges for **low stock** and **Serial**.

### 7.2 Create a category

1. Select **New category**.
2. Enter a **Category name** (for example "Phones") and choose a **Type** (General, Electronics, Clothing & Apparel, Automotive, Food & Beverage, Office Supplies or Other). Add a **Description** if you like.
3. Choose how stock is tracked:
   - Tick **Use serial numbers** if every unit has its own serial number. Each unit gets its own row with a **Serial Number** and **Product model**.
   - Leave it off to track a **Quantity** instead.
4. Optional settings:
   - **Organize with subcategories**: products go into subcategories instead of directly into this category.
   - **Track profit** (owner only): adds a **Cost price** column so Storvv can show profit.
   - **Department access**: tick departments to limit who can see this category. Leave all unticked for everyone.
5. Set up the **Table template**, the columns each product will have:
   - **Product** and **Unit price** are always included.
   - Select **Add column** to add your own. Give it a label, pick a type (**Text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Select**, **Yes / no** or **Currency**) and tick **Required** if it must be filled in.
   - Or select **Import Excel** to create columns from the headings of a spreadsheet.
6. Select **Create category**.

If you are the owner and didn't tick Track profit, Storvv asks whether to continue without it. You can turn it on later.

### 7.3 Subcategories

If a category uses subcategories:

1. Open the category.
2. Select **Add subcategory**.
3. Enter a **Subcategory name** (for example "Corolla") and optional **Description**.
4. Select **Create subcategory**.

Subcategories copy the parent's columns and settings. Open a subcategory to add products to it.

### 7.4 Edit, duplicate or delete a category

Select the **⋯** button on a category card:

- **Edit**: change any setting. If the category has subcategories, Storvv asks whether to apply column and access changes to them too (**Parent only** or **Apply to subcategories**).
- **Duplicate** (Medium and Enterprise): create one or more new categories with the same columns and settings, but no products. Enter the new names and select **Duplicate**.
- **Delete**: permanently deletes the category, its subcategories and every product inside. Tick the confirmation box and select **Delete category**.

To delete several at once, tick their checkboxes, then select **Delete** in the bar that appears.

### 7.5 Copy categories from another branch (Enterprise, owner)

Useful when opening a new branch with the same kind of stock.

1. Switch to the branch you want to copy **into**.
2. On Inventory, select **Copy from branch**.
3. Choose the **Source branch**.
4. Tick the categories to copy (all are ticked at first). Optionally tick **Also copy subcategories into selected categories**.
5. Choose what happens if a category with the same name already exists: **Skip it** or **Add a suffix** ("(copy)").
6. Select **Copy**.

Only the category setup is copied, not the products. Department restrictions are not copied.

### 7.6 Add products

Open a category (or subcategory) and select **Add product**.

**Quantity-tracked category**

1. Fill in the fields, such as Product, Unit price and Quantity.
2. If you're the owner and the category tracks profit, enter the **Unit cost** (what you paid). Storvv shows the estimated margin.
3. Select **Add product**.

**Serial-tracked category** (add many units at once)

1. Fill in the **Shared product details** once, such as Product model and Unit price.
2. Under **Serial numbers**, select **Add serial number** for each unit and type its serial.
3. Select **Add products**. Each serial becomes its own product row.

Storvv won't let you add the same serial number twice in a category.

### 7.7 Import and export products with Excel

**Import**
1. Inside a category, select **Import** and choose an Excel file (.xlsx or .xls).
2. The first row must contain column headings that match your category's columns.
3. Storvv adds the rows and tells you how many were imported and how many were skipped (for example duplicates or invalid values).

**Export**
- Select **Export** to download all products in the category as an Excel file.

### 7.8 Working with products

Inside a category you can:

- **Search products** by any value, such as name, SKU or serial.
- **Sort** by Name, Unit price, SKU, Date in or Status.
- Switch between **Table view** and **Shelf view** (cards).
- On a computer, **click a cell to edit it** directly. Press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.

Select **⋯** on a product for:

- **View details**: all of the product's information.
- **History**: everything that happened to this product, such as when it was added, discounted, sold or returned.
- **Edit**: change its details.
- **Add discount** / **Change discount**: take a percentage or fixed amount off the price. You'll see a preview of the new price before you select **Apply discount**.
- **Stock loan** (Enterprise, serial categories): lend it to a reseller. See [Stock loans](#12-stock-loans).
- **Duplicate** (Medium and Enterprise): copy the product. For serial items, enter the new serial numbers.
- **Delete**: remove it permanently. An **Undo** option appears for a few seconds.

To act on several products at once, tick them and use **Discount**, **Stock loan** or **Delete** in the bar that appears.

### 7.9 Product status

Each product shows its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Available** | In stock and ready to sell. |
| **On stock loan** | Lent to a reseller. |
| **Awaiting payment** | Held for a customer who has paid a deposit. |
| **Sold** | Sold on a receipt. |
| **Returned** | Came back through a refund. |

Products that are sold, on loan or awaiting payment are **locked**: they can't be edited, discounted, duplicated or deleted. This keeps your sales records accurate.

The **Source** column shows where a product came from: normal **Stock**, a **Swap-in** (trade-in on a sale) or a **Buyback**.

### 7.10 Low stock and reorder lists

- Set your alert level in **Settings → Inventory → Low stock alert** (default 10).
- Categories with low stock show a "**N low**" badge, and appear in the **Low stock** tab.
- Select **Export reorder list** to download an Excel list of everything running low, with a suggested reorder quantity and a space for notes to your supplier.

## 8. Selling

There are two ways to sell:

- **New sale**: the full checkout, with customer details, deposits, split payments and trade-ins.
- **Quick sale**: a fast till for walk-in customers, with barcode scanning.

Both are on the **Sales** page, and **New sale** is also on the Overview.

### 8.1 Make a sale with New sale

1. Select **New sale**. A panel opens with four steps: **Category**, **Subcategory**, **Products**, **Checkout**.

**Step 1: Category**

2. Select the category you're selling from, then **Next**. (If it has no subcategories, Storvv skips straight to Products.)

**Step 2: Subcategory**

3. Select the subcategory, then **Next**.

**Step 3: Products**

4. Tick the products the customer is buying. Only available stock is shown.
   - For quantity items, set the **Quantity**. Storvv warns you if you ask for more than is in stock.
   - Select **Discount** on a line to take money off that item.
   - To add products from a different category to the same sale, select **Add from another category**.
5. Check the **Selected** summary and the **Total**, then select **Next**.

**Step 4: Checkout**

6. Enter the **Customer name** (required). As you type, Storvv suggests existing customers; pick one to fill in their details. Add **Customer email**, **Customer phone** and **Customer address** if you have them.
7. Choose the **Payment method**, such as Cash, Card (POS) or Bank Transfer.
   - Customer paying in more than one way? Tick **Split payment**, then add a line for each method and amount. The amounts must add up to the total; Storvv shows what's left to allocate.
8. Choose the payment type:
   - **Paid in full**: the stock is marked sold straight away.
   - **Balance due**: enter the **Deposit collected today**. The stock is held for the customer and the sale appears under **Outstanding** until it's paid off.
9. Add **Notes** if needed. If you gave a discount, enter a **Discount reason**.
10. For a trade-in, tick **This is a swap-in transaction** (see 8.3).
11. Check the totals and select **Create sale**.

The sale appears in your Sales list, the stock is updated and the customer is saved.

### 8.2 Make a sale with Quick sale

1. Select **Quick sale**.
2. Choose the **Category** you're selling from.
3. Add products in any of these ways:
   - Select **Scan** and point your camera at the barcode or QR code. Each scan adds the item. Select **Stop** when done.
   - Type a barcode, SKU or serial and select **Search**.
   - Tap a product in the list.
4. In **Selected items**, use **−** and **+** to change quantities. Use **Add discount** on a line if needed (a discount reason is then required).
5. Optionally open **Customer info** to add a name and phone. Without a name, the sale is recorded as "Walk-in Customer".
6. Owner only: open **Commission** to record a commission owed to someone, such as a referral agent.
7. Under **Payment**, tap the payment method. For more than one method, turn on **Split payment**.
8. Select **Complete sale**.

Quick sales are always paid in full. Keep each quick sale to one category.

**Note from your manager:** if the owner has added a note for cashiers on the branch (for example "10% off accessories today"), it appears at the top of Quick sale and New sale.

### 8.3 Trade-ins (swap-in)

When a customer gives you an item as part payment:

1. In New sale, at Checkout, tick **This is a swap-in transaction**.
2. Choose the **Category for swapped-in device**.
3. Fill in the device details, including its value.
4. The value is taken off the total as **Swap credit (trade-in)**. The total is shown as **Amount due**.
5. Select **Create sale**.

The traded-in item is added to your stock in the chosen category, marked as a Swap-in.

### 8.4 The Sales page

Open **Sales** from the menu.

- **Numbers** at the top: sales in this branch, completed value, today, this month and outstanding.
- Two tabs: **Sales** and **Outstanding**.
- **Search sales** by receipt number or customer.
- Filter by status (**Completed**, **Pending**, **Refunded**) and date (**Today**, **This week**, **This month**).
- Select any column heading to sort.

Select a sale to open its details. Select **⋮** on a row for **View sale**, **History**, **Refund** and **Delete**.

**Sale statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Completed** | Paid in full. Stock is marked sold. |
| **Balance due** | Deposit paid, balance still owed. Stock is held. Shown under Outstanding. |
| **Refunded** | Returned. Items went back into stock. |
| **Cancelled** | An outstanding order was cancelled and its stock released. |
| **Pending** | Used by older sales only. |

### 8.5 View, print and email a receipt

1. Select a sale to open **Sale details**. You'll see the items, payment and customer.
2. Select **Preview receipt** to see the full receipt with your logo, business details, items, totals, payment and policies.
3. From the receipt you can:
   - **Print / PDF**: download the receipt as a PDF to print or send.
   - **Email**: enter the customer's **Email address** and select **Send**. The PDF is attached.
   - **Copy number**: copy the receipt number.

Owners also see the cost of goods and gross profit on completed sales.

### 8.6 Take payment on a balance

1. Go to **Sales → Outstanding**, or open the sale's details.
2. Select **Record payment**.
3. Enter the **Payment amount**, or use **Pay balance** or **Half**.
4. Choose the **Payment method**.
5. Select **Record payment** (for a part payment) or **Complete & move to sales** (when it pays off the balance).

When the balance is fully paid, the sale becomes **Completed**, the held stock is marked sold, and the sale moves to the Sales tab.

### 8.7 Cancel an outstanding order

If a customer with a balance due won't be completing the purchase:

1. On **Sales → Outstanding**, select **Cancel sale** (or **Cancel order** in the sale's details).
2. Confirm.

The order is marked **Cancelled** and the held stock becomes available again. You need the **Refund & cancel orders** permission.

### 8.8 Refund a sale

1. Open the sale and select **Refund**, or use **⋮ → Refund** on the Sales list.
2. Check the sale information and the items to be returned.
3. Optionally enter a **Return reason**.
4. Tick the confirmation box and select **Confirm return / refund**.

The sale becomes **Refunded** and every item goes back into stock. Refunds are for the whole sale. You need the **Refund & cancel orders** permission.

### 8.9 Delete a sale (owner only)

Use this only for mistakes. To reverse a real sale, refund it instead.

1. Select **⋮ → Delete** on the sale.
2. Read what will happen: items go back into stock, and the customer is removed if this was their only sale.
3. Tick the confirmation box and select **Delete sale**.
4. An **Undo** option appears for 5 seconds.

### 8.10 Sale history

Select **⋮ → History** on a sale to see when it was recorded and whether it was refunded.

## 9. Customers

Customers are added **automatically** when you put a name, phone number or email on a sale. There is no separate form to fill in.

### 9.1 The Customers page

- **Numbers**: Customers, Repeat customers, Average spend and, on Medium and Enterprise, **Owed to you**.
- Tabs: **All**, **Repeat** and (Medium and Enterprise) **Owe money**.
- **Search customers** by name, phone or email.
- **Sort** by Recent, Name, Orders or Spent.

### 9.2 See a customer's details

Select a customer to see:

- Their phone (tap to call), email (tap to email) and address.
- **Orders**, **Total spent** and **Last order**.
- Every sale they've made. Select a sale to open it.

Customer details are updated when you enter new details on their next sale.

### 9.3 Customer balance (Medium and Enterprise)

A simple ledger for customers who buy on credit or pay you in advance.

1. Open a customer and select **Manage balance** (or **⋮ → Manage balance**).
2. Choose **Add charge** to increase what they owe, or **Record payment** to reduce it.
3. Enter the **Amount** and an optional **Note**.
4. Select **Save**.

**Recent activity** shows the latest entries. This ledger is separate from balance-due sales: to settle a balance-due sale, use **Record payment** on the Sales page.

## 10. Buybacks

Use Buybacks when **you buy an item from a customer**. You pay them, and the item goes straight into your stock at the price you paid. (If the item is part payment for a sale happening now, use a swap-in instead.)

### 10.1 Record a buyback

1. Open **Buybacks** and select **Record buyback**.
2. Enter the **Customer name**, and optionally their **Phone** and **Email**.
3. Choose the **Inventory category** the item belongs in.
4. Fill in the item details (these are the category's columns).
5. Enter the **Amount paid to customer** and the **Payment method**.
6. Add **Notes** if useful, for example condition or ID checked.
7. Select **Record buyback**.

The item is added to the chosen category with its cost set to what you paid, and marked as a Buyback.

### 10.2 The Buybacks list

Shows the number of buybacks and total paid. Each row shows the item, customer, amount paid, payment method and date. Select the item to find it in Inventory. Use **Search buybacks** to find one by customer, phone or item.

## 11. Sales leads

*Medium and Enterprise.*

A lead is someone who asked about a product but hasn't bought yet: a walk-in, a phone call or a WhatsApp message. Leads help you follow up and turn interest into sales.

### 11.1 Add a lead

1. Open **Sales leads** and select **Add lead**.
2. Enter the **Customer name**, and optionally **Phone** and **Email**. Storvv warns you if there's already an open lead for the same contact.
3. Enter the **Product interest**, what they're looking for.
4. Optionally **Link inventory item**: search your stock and select **Find item** to attach a specific product.
5. Add an **Estimated value**, choose the **Source** (Walk-in, Phone, WhatsApp, Referral or Other) and add **Notes**.
6. Select **Save lead**.

### 11.2 Follow up

Open a lead to work on it:

- Change its **Status** as things move: **New** → **Contacted** → **Negotiating**.
- Set **Assigned to** to give it to a team member.
- Add notes under **Add a note** and select **Save note**.
- The **Activity** section shows everything that has happened.

On the leads list you can also change an open lead's status directly in the table.

### 11.3 Turn a lead into a sale

1. Open the lead and select **Create sale**.
2. The New sale panel opens with the customer's details and the product already filled in.
3. Finish the sale as normal.

The lead is marked **Won** with the sale amount.

### 11.4 Close a lead that didn't buy

1. Open the lead and select **Mark as lost**.
2. Optionally give a **Reason** (for example price or bought elsewhere).
3. Select **Mark as lost**.

Lost leads stay visible under the **Lost** tab.

### 11.5 Lead tabs

**Open** (default), **All**, **Won** and **Lost**. The numbers at the top show open leads, their possible value and how many were won.

## 12. Stock loans

*Enterprise. Serial-tracked categories only.*

A stock loan is when you give serial-numbered items to a reseller or partner to sell for you. The items stay yours until the reseller sells them or brings them back.

### 12.1 Lend items

1. Go to **Inventory** and open a serial-tracked category.
2. Tick the products to lend, then select **Stock loan** in the bar. (Or use **⋯ → Stock loan** on one product.)
3. Enter the **Borrower name**, and optionally **Phone** and **Notes**.
4. Select **Confirm stock loan**.

The items show as **On stock loan** and are locked until the loan is closed.

### 12.2 Close a loan

Open **Stock loans** from the menu. Select **⋮** on a loan:

- **Mark sold by borrower**: the reseller sold the items. They become **Sold** in your inventory.
- **Return to stock**: the items came back. They become **Available** again.

If you sell a loaned item yourself through a sale, the loan is updated automatically.

### 12.3 The Stock loans list

- **Numbers**: On loan, Units out, Sold by borrower, Returned.
- Tabs: **On loan**, **Sold by borrower**, **Returned**, **All**.
- Select the units count on a row to see exactly which items are on that loan.

## 13. Transfers between branches

*Enterprise, owner.*

Move stock from one branch to another, with an approval step and optional shipment tracking. Stock only moves when you mark the transfer complete.

### 13.1 Request a transfer

1. Open **Transfers** and stay on **New transfer**.
2. Choose the **From branch** (sending) and **To branch** (receiving).
3. Under **Take items from category**, choose the category in the sending branch.
4. Under **Put items in category**, choose where the items should go in the receiving branch.
5. Choose the items:
   - Serial items: tick each unit.
   - Quantity items: type how many to **Move**.
   Items that are sold, on loan or already in another transfer aren't shown.
6. Add **Notes** for the other branch if needed.
7. Select **Request transfer**.

The transfer is created as **Pending approval**. The items are reserved, but nothing has moved yet.

### 13.2 Approve, track and complete

Go to the **History** tab and select **⋮** on the transfer:

1. **Approve**: the transfer becomes **In transit**.
2. **Add tracking** (optional): enter the **Carrier** and **Tracking number**.
3. **Complete**: when the stock arrives. The items are removed from the sending branch and added to the receiving branch.

If some items can't be moved (for example one was sold in the meantime), the transfer finishes as **Completed (partial)** and tells you how many moved.

To stop a transfer, select **Cancel transfer**. No stock moves.

### 13.3 Transfer statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Pending approval** | Requested. Items reserved. Nothing has moved. |
| **In transit** | Approved and on its way. |
| **Completed** | Stock has moved to the receiving branch. |
| **Completed (partial)** | Some items moved; others couldn't. |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped. No stock moved. |

### 13.4 Branch reports

The **Branch reports** tab compares sales across all your branches.

1. Choose a **Date range** (7 days to all time) and a **Branch** (or all).
2. See **Revenue**, **Sales**, **Items sold** and **Average sale**, and a table by branch.
3. Select **Export** to download a PDF.

### 14.2 Report tabs

**Overview**

## 15. Team: departments, staff and permissions

*Owner only.*

Departments group your staff (for example Sales or Warehouse). Each staff member belongs to one department in a branch, and you decide exactly what they can do.

### 15.1 Create a department

1. Open **Team** and select **New department**.
2. Choose a **Department type** (Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Inventory, Warehouse, Finance, Accounting, Human Resources, Operations, IT or Other).
3. Enter a **Department name** and optional **Description**.
4. Select **Create department**.

Use **⋮** on a department to **Open**, **Edit** or **Delete** it.

### 15.2 Add a staff member

1. Open the department and select **Add staff**.
2. Enter **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Position** and optional **Phone**.
3. Storvv creates a secure password for them automatically.
   - Leave **Email sign-in details** ticked to email it to them.
   - Or untick it to copy the password yourself after the account is created.
   - **Regenerate password** creates a different one.
4. Set the **Hire date**, optional **Salary** and **Status** (Active, Inactive or On leave).
5. Set their **Permissions** (see 15.3).
6. Select **Add staff**.

You'll see **Invite emailed**, or **Account created** with the password to copy and share. On first sign-in the staff member is asked to choose their own password.

**Pending sign-in invites** at the top of the department lists people who haven't set their own password yet. From there you can **Email** their details again, **Copy invite** (a ready-to-send message) or copy the password.

### 15.3 Permissions

For each area, choose how much the person can do:

| Level | What it allows |
| ----- | -------------- |
| **None** | No access to this area. |
| **View** | Can look but not change anything. |
| **Manage** | Can add, edit and delete. |
| **Custom** | Pick exactly which actions: **Create**, **Edit**, **Delete**. |

The areas are:

- **Inventory**: categories, products, quantities and prices.
- **Receipts**: sales. An extra option, **Refund & cancel orders**, controls whether they can refund sales and cancel outstanding orders. Turn it on separately.
- **Sales leads**: the enquiry pipeline.
- **Customer buybacks**: recording items bought from customers.
- **Stock loans**: always on for staff. Add **Can delete** if needed.
- **Multi-store sync**: transfers and branch reports.

New staff start with: view inventory, view and create sales, view/create/edit leads, and view/create/edit stock loans.

Staff only see categories that are open to everyone or limited to their department.

### 15.4 Edit, move, remove or reactivate staff

Select **⋮** on a staff member:

- **Edit**: change their details or permissions.
- **Move to another department**: choose the **Destination department** and confirm. Their access follows the new department.
- **Remove from department**: they can no longer sign in. Their past sales and activity still show their name.

Removed staff appear on the **Removed** tab. Select **⋮ → Reactivate** to let them sign in again with their existing email and password.

If you have two-factor authentication on, Storvv asks for your code before removing or reactivating staff.

## 24. Appendix: known gaps (for the Storvv team)

While writing this guide, these differences between what the app says and what it does were found in the current code. The guide above only describes behaviour that works. These are worth fixing or hiding:

**Sales and receipts**
1. **Settings → Sales & receipts** (**Receipt prefix**, **Next receipt number**, **Print receipts automatically**) is saved but not used. Receipt numbers are always "REC-" plus 6 digits, and Print / PDF always downloads a PDF.
2. Receipts have no WhatsApp or share-link button, although the server supports both. The public receipt page (`/r/[token]`) also always shows amounts in naira.
3. Refunds are full refunds only. The refund overwrites the sale's notes. The confirmation says the customer will be notified, but no notification is sent.
4. "Pending" is offered as a filter but no screen creates pending sales. Cancelled sales have no filter.
5. The Sales search label says it searches items, but it only matches receipt number, customer name and email.
6. Quick sale only updates stock for the currently selected category. Changing category mid-sale can leave items unsold in stock.
7. Quick sale commission is added to the saved total, but the **Complete sale** button shows the total without it.
8. The receipt preview shows the raw status "Balance_due".
9. Staff with the **Delete** chip see Delete on sales, but the server only allows the owner to delete.
10. The **Create** chip for Receipts isn't enforced: anyone signed in can make sales, and the Sales menu shows even when Receipts is set to None.
11. After **Create sale** there's no success message, and errors use a browser alert.

**Customers**
12. A balance-due sale doesn't add to the customer's balance ledger, and recording a ledger payment doesn't settle an outstanding sale. Users may expect these to be linked.
13. Customers can't be edited or deleted directly.

**Inventory**
14. The **Select** column type has no way to enter its options in the category drawer, so it behaves like a text field unless created from an Excel import.
15. There's no way to remove a discount once applied.
16. Inventory **Delete** permission is not checked anywhere. Deleting categories follows **Create**, deleting products follows **Edit**.
17. The Inventory menu item isn't hidden for staff with Inventory set to None.

**Access and navigation**
18. **Transfers**: the permission panel offers Multi-store sync to staff, but only the owner can request transfers or see history.
19. **Reports**: the menu shows Reports to any staff member with a Manage permission, but the page only lets in legacy "manager" staff.
20. In Growing business mode, **Branches** shows on Micro even though Micro allows only one branch. The plan-limit message always says "Storvv Micro allows 1 store", even on Medium.
21. The account menu shows **Activity logs** to staff on Medium/Enterprise based on plan alone.

**Other**
22. The password reset page accepts 6-character passwords; sign-up and change password require 12.
23. **Active sessions → Revoke** only removes the device from the list; it doesn't sign that device out.
24. **Notification channels** are saved on the device only.
25. The Reports **Excel** button downloads a CSV file, not an .xlsx.
26. **Search → Suggested** shows Payment links even though it's coming soon.
